Project Overview
About
"TalentBase" is an intuitive job search platform designed to connect job seekers with employers efficiently, featuring advanced filtering, personalized recommendations and real-time notifications for a seamless job hunting experience.
Duration
06 Months
The Problem
Job seekers often struggle to find relevant job opportunities due to the lack of efficient filtering options, personalized recommendations and real-time updates, leading to a time-consuming and frustating job hunting experience.
The Goal
The goal of 'TalentBase' is to streamline the job search process by providing an intuitive platform with advanced filtering options, personalized recommendations and real-time notifications, ensuring a seamless and efficient connection between job seekers and employers.

Understanding the User
User Research - Summery
For the “TalentBase” project, we conducted a comprehensive user research study involving both qualitative and quantitative methods. Initially, we conducted online surveys and in-depth interviews with job seekers and employers to gather insights into their needs, behaviors, and pain points. We also analyzed existing job portals to identify common usability issues and opportunities for improvement. Our initial assumption was that job seekers primarily struggled with finding relevant job postings quickly, while employers faced challenges in managing a high volume of applications. However, our research revealed that job seekers also highly value personalized job recommendations and real-time notifications, while employers prioritized tools for efficient candidate filtering and communication. These insights significantly shaped our design strategy, leading us to focus on creating a more personalized, efficient, and user-friendly job search and recruitment experience.
User Research - Pain Points
  • Difficulty in Finding Relevant Jobs
  • Lack of Personalized Recommendations
  • Inefficient Application Tracking
  • Employer Recruitment Challenges

Starting the Design
Information Architecture (Sitemap)
Difficulty with website navigation was a primary pain point for users, so I used that knowledge to create a sitemap. 
My goal here was to make strategic information architecture decisions that would improve overall website navigation. The structure I chose was designed to make things simple and easy. 
Paper Wireframes
Next, I sketched out paper wireframes for each screen in my app, keeping the user pain points about navigation, browsing, and ‘apply for a job’ flow in mind.
The home screen paper wireframe variations to the right focus on optimizing the browsing experience for users.

Refining the Design
Mockups
Based on the insights from the usability study, I made changes to improve the job search. One of the changes I made was adding the advanced filter option to search jobs. This allowed users more freedom to search relevant jobs without going through a long list of irrelevant jobs.
To view job listings and courses easier, I added ‘List View’ & ‘Grid View’ options to job listings page and courses page.
Mockups - Original Screen Size
Mockups - Screen Size Variations
I included considerations for additional screen sizes in my mockups based on my earlier wireframes. Because users search jobs from a variety of devices, I felt it was important to optimize the browsing experience for a range of device sizes, such as mobile and tablet so users have the smoothest experience possible. 
High-Fidelity (Hi-Fi) Prototype
My hi-fi prototype followed the same user flow as the lo-fi prototype, and included the design changes made after the usability study, as well as several changes suggested by members of my team.

Takeaways
Impact:
Our target users shared that the design was intuitive to navigate through, more engaging with the images, and demonstrated a clear visual hierarchy.
What I learned:
I learned that even a small design change can have a huge impact on the user experience. The most important takeaway for me is to always focus on the real needs of the user when coming up with design ideas and solutions.
